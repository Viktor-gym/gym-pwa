These 18 3D animations are from the 22-exercise free preview. Four movements
already have exact animations in the Vital Free50 set and are not duplicated.
All source frames are used with their original timing, composited on white,
resized to 480px and encoded as muted H.264 at 24fps for phone compatibility.
Posters are a single extracted frame. No generative restyling was performed.
