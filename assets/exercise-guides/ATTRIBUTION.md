# Illustrated Exercise Guides

112 Ukrainian illustrated sheets supplied by the project owner from their
ChatGPT-generated materials, selected by exact exercise catalog ID. Where two
sheets existed for one exercise, the newer source creation date was selected.
Images were compressed to WebP without changing their contents.

These are illustrative technique reminders, not medical guidance, manufacturer
manuals, or a clinically validated measurement of muscle recruitment.
Existing exercise-data and third-party animation attributions remain unchanged.
