v50 used this source for three-position illustrations. The owner rejected that
presentation after testing. v51 restores the original photo pairs and removes
all 261 sprite/poster pairs and their playback code. The 68 smooth 3D animations
are unchanged and come from the separately credited sources.

Only the metadata of 178 additional variants remains in exercise-legacy-v51.js,
so exercises already saved by users retain their catalogId references. These
variants are not offered as new library suggestions. No exact photo is guessed.
