# App-Integrated 3D Demonstrations

These 50 demonstrations come from the **Vital Animations Free50 pack**, accessed
on 2026-10-27. They are rendered 3D characters, not footage of real people.

- Official free pack: https://vitalanimations.com/free-pack
- Publisher statement and usage terms: https://github.com/exercisedb-pro/exercisedb-dataset
- Publisher license: https://vitalanimations.com/license

The publisher explicitly offers the free pack for use inside developers' own
projects under its commercial usage terms, including self-hosted apps/websites.
This does **not** make the artwork open-source or public domain. The assets are
incorporated into GymTracker's exercise guide, not offered as a reusable dataset.
Do not redistribute the original pack, sell the raw assets, or assume the app's
code license grants rights to this artwork. No paid assets or purchases are used.

App versions are resized to 480px, muted, transcoded to H.264 with fast-start,
and paired with a single extracted JPEG poster. MP4 is only the playback
container; the content itself is animated 3D motion. Posters stay still in lists.

Muscle highlighting is a separate informational map, not an overlay painted
onto the animation. Metadata for existing catalog entries remains from Free
Exercise DB (see ../exercises/SOURCE.md); additional entries use Free50 metadata.
Only exact named movement/equipment variants are linked. Missing animations
remain clearly labelled static references rather than a substitute movement.
